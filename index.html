About Me

Introduction

My name is Abhishree Acharya. I am currently a Class 11 student with a strong interest in learning and exploring new ideas. I would describe myself as hardworking, ambitious, quiet and observant. I usually prefer listening carefully before sharing my own thoughts, as I believe that listening helps a person understand others more clearly.

I have always been interested in learning about things that are new to me. I enjoy discovering information that makes me look at familiar things from a different perspective. I am also curious about how people think, what influences their decisions and why different people can have different opinions about the same situation.

I am someone who likes to observe, think and understand before making conclusions. Although I can sometimes be lazy, I try to stay focused when something is important to me. I believe that knowing my weaknesses is just as important as recognising my strengths because it gives me an opportunity to improve myself.

Another quality I value is curiosity. I believe that there is always something new to learn, whether it comes from books, people, experiences or everyday life. This curiosity encourages me to explore different subjects and develop a broader understanding of the world around me.

Education

Academic Background

I am currently studying at St. Xavier's College in Class 11 under the Science stream. I have chosen Computer Science as one of my subjects because of my growing interest in computers and technology.

My education has provided me with opportunities to study different subjects and develop a more logical and analytical way of thinking. I enjoy subjects that require understanding concepts and applying knowledge rather than simply memorising information.

Studying in the Science stream has also helped me understand the importance of accuracy, patience and regular practice. Each subject presents different challenges, and dealing with those challenges has helped me become more responsible towards my studies.

Computer Science is one of the areas that interests me the most. I have basic knowledge of the C programming language and enjoy learning how instructions written by a programmer can be used to solve problems and perform different tasks.

Interest in Computer Science

I find Computer Science interesting because it combines logical thinking with creativity. A computer can follow a set of instructions and perform complicated tasks, but those instructions begin with a simple idea created by a person.

Even simple concepts can be represented using symbols. For example, H2O represents water, while x2 represents x squared. Learning such concepts has helped me appreciate how different forms of information can be represented in a clear and meaningful way.
